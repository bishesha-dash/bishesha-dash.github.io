Planetary Gearbox Design

Gear DesignKISSsoftMachining

A ground-up single-stage planetary gearbox: 6:1 ratio, sized from AGMA bending and contact stress first principles, then verified in KISSsoft and built.

Overview

The problem & the approach

Rather than pick a catalogue gearbox, this project designs one from scratch: ratio selection, tooth counts satisfying assembly constraints, profile shift optimisation, bearing sizing and a machinable housing.

The 72/24/24 tooth combination balances load sharing across three planets. Profile shift coefficients were tuned to equalise bending strength between sun and planet, the classic weak point in small planetaries.

Highlights

  • AGMA hand calculations cross-checked against KISSsoft models
  • Profile shift tuned to balance sun/planet bending strength
  • Housing machined in two setups on a 3-axis mill

Analysis & results

Rated at 45 Nm output with a 1.4 application factor, the limiting mode is planet tooth bending — KISSsoft safety factors agree with AGMA hand calcs within 6%.

Measured backlash of 14 arc-minutes and 96.5% efficiency at rated load closely matched predictions from the mesh loss model.
