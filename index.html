01you

Why you mean so much to me

  1. i.

    You make ordinary days worth remembering.

    A random Tuesday with you somehow turns into a story I’m still telling weeks later.

  2. ii.

    You know the real, messy me — and you stay.

    You’ve seen all my worst habits (exhibit A: the reason this website exists) and you still choose me. I don’t take that lightly.

  3. iii.

    You’re the smartest person I know.

    You design machines that earn their tolerances. Somehow you’ve tolerated me too — which might be your most impressive work yet.

  4. iv.

    You make me want to be better.

    Not because you ask me to. Because you deserve someone who shows up, every single time.

  5. v.

    You feel like home.

    It doesn’t matter where we are. If you’re there, I’m okay.

  6. vi.

    Your laugh.

    That’s it. That’s the whole reason. It fixes everything.

03me

I know I don’t always get it right.

I forget things. Dates, texts, the thing you told me twice. Sometimes I get so lost in my own head that I forget to show you what you mean to me — and you should never have to wonder about that.

Things I forget

  • Our anniversary. (Clearly.)
  • To text back when I get busy
  • To say thank you for the little things
  • That it’s milk chocolate only. (Dark doesn’t count.)

Things I’ll never forget

  • The way you say my name
  • How easy it is to be myself around you
  • Every time you showed up for me without being asked
  • How your hand fits in mine

I’m so lucky
to have you.

Not lucky like finding money in an old jacket. Lucky like the universe made a mistake in my favour — and I plan to spend a very long time deserving it.

So, a few promises

  1. Our anniversary now lives in my head — and, just to be safe, in three calendars, two alarms and a sticky note on my mirror.
  2. When you’re talking, my phone goes face-down.
  3. I’ll plan the next date — all of it, including where we eat.
  4. I’ll say “I love you” out loud more often, instead of just thinking it really hard.
